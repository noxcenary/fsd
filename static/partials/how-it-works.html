2 Deterministic Dispatch Model

Multicriteria AI Shortage Matching Algorithm

Crisis demand cannot depend on opaque neural networks. ReliefNet matches inventory requisitions using a transparent, weighted formula with full parameter explainability.

Formula Equation: Score $S_m \in [0, 100]$
$S_m = (0.35 \times Q) + (0.25 \times D) + (0.20 \times V) + (0.20 \times R)$
Quantity Match & Readiness State (Q) 35% Weight
Evaluates surplus batch size vs. requested shortage, storage packaging, temperature sensitivity, and immediate pick-up readiness.
Distance & Real-Time Transit Logistics (D) 25% Weight
Kilometers to incident cluster, road passability indices (NDMA landslide/flood overlays), and specialized vehicle availability.
Verification Tier Level (V) 20% Weight
Tier-1 Statutory Verified yields 100 base pts; Tier-2 Community Endorsed yields 75 base pts. Discourages speculative unverified listings.
Historical Reliability & Settlement Rate (R) 20% Weight
Past 12 months fulfillment ratio, zero unexcused cancellations, barcode scan integrity, and consignee sign-off accuracy.

Incident Handling: Non-Linear Exception States

State: DISPUTED
Arbitration Required

Triggered automatically when received goods vary by >2% in unit weight, carton quantity, or fail batch expiration thresholds.

Arbitration Routine: Both org manifests freeze. Digital photographic manifest uploaded to State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA) monitor panel within 60 minutes for weighbridge conciliation.
State: CANCELLED
Grace Periods

Permitted without reputation penalty only during States 1 (Requested) and 2 (Accepted) prior to fleet dispatch.

Reputation Protection: Late cancellations in State 3 trigger an immediate automatic 30-day reliability penalty (-15 pts) unless certified flood/landslide roadblock manifest is confirmed via GIS feed.
4 Trust Foundations

Verification vs. Reputation: Core Governance Distinction

A common failure in mutual aid platforms is conflating identity credentials with operational competence. ReliefNet strictly separates the two primitives.

Dimension Verification ("Who You Are") Reputation ("What You Do")
System Purpose Identity barrier to prevent fraud, predatory cartels, and sybil organizations. Operational score measuring fulfillment accuracy, turnaround speed, and integrity.
Mutability Static / Discrete: Tier-1, Tier-2, or Unverified. Updated only on credential renewal. Dynamic / Rolling: Continuous score out of 100 based on the last 30 completed transfers.
Assessment Factor Registry documents, government API records, physical headquarters audit, bank validity. Ontime dispatches, QR confirmation rates, low dispute ratios, packing standards.
Authority Impact Unlocks platform access to emergency shortage marketplace. Determines algorithmic matching priority in high-demand surge crises.
Can It Drop? Only upon legal dissolution, statutory revocation, or proven security breach. Yes. Incomplete dispatches, unannounced cargo drops, or unresolved disputes degrade score.
